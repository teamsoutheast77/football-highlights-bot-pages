Football Highlights Bot - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

1. Introduction

Football Highlights Bot is a personal automation application designed to discover football highlights from official and authorised YouTube channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how the application accesses, uses, stores and protects information obtained through Google's YouTube Data API.

2. Information Accessed

When the application owner authorises Football Highlights Bot using Google OAuth 2.0, the application may access:

  • YouTube account and channel information.
  • YouTube playlist information and video identifiers.
  • Public YouTube video metadata, including titles and publication dates.
  • OAuth authentication credentials required for API access.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate the application owner.
  • Discover football highlights from authorised YouTube channels.
  • Identify videos already present in the owner's playlist.
  • Add relevant football highlights to the owner's private YouTube playlist.

The application does not intentionally collect information for advertising, profiling or marketing.

4. Data Storage and Security

OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on the application owner's computer to enable scheduled operation.

Authentication credentials are excluded from the application's Git repository and are not intentionally published or shared.

Operational logs may be stored locally for troubleshooting and monitoring.

5. Data Sharing

Football Highlights Bot does not sell or intentionally disclose Google account information or OAuth tokens to third parties.

The application communicates with Google's services to perform authorised YouTube API operations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Authentication tokens remain on the application owner's computer until deleted, replaced or revoked.

The application owner can remove locally stored tokens and operational logs at any time.

7. Revoking Access

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account Connections .

8. YouTube API Services

Football Highlights Bot uses YouTube API Services. By using the application, the owner is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service .

Google's Privacy Policy is available at Google Privacy Policy .

10. Changes to This Policy
